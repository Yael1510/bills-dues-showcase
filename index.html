How a month goes

Add it once. Get reminded. Tap Pay.

Recurring bills roll over to the next due date by themselves, so after the first setup you only open the app when something is due.

Add your bills

Name, amount, due date, how often it repeats

Pick a category and choose one-time, monthly, quarterly or yearly. For loans and credit cards, also enter the loan amount and what's left, and the app tracks the balance for you.

  • Utilities, loans & cards, subscriptions, rent, dues & fees
  • Notes for the account number or where to pay
See what's due

The dashboard answers "what do I pay this week?"

Four numbers up top: overdue, due in the next 7 days, left to pay this month and paid this month. Tap any of them to see the bills behind the number. Below, every bill that's overdue or due within 3 days, color coded, then everything coming up in the next 30 days.

See where it goes

Your monthly costs, in one chart

The Bills tab shows what each category costs you per month. Tap a category to see only those bills, and tap it again to see them all. Switch it to how bills are paid, and the chart follows.

Mark it paid

One tap, and next month is already set

Tap Pay, check the amount, the date and how you paid (GCash, bank or auto-debit), and confirm. The payment goes into your history, a recurring bill moves to its next due date, and a loan's balance goes down.

Look back

History and reports, without a spreadsheet

Tap any bill to see its balance, payoff date and the full schedule of remaining due dates. History keeps every payment, grouped by month, with late ones flagged. Reports show what you paid each month and what each category costs you. Export to CSV for Excel anytime.

For the whole household

Share the bills. See who paid what.

Create a shared dashboard, like “Household” or “Condo”, and invite family by email. Your private bills stay private.

  • Everyone can view, add, edit and mark bills paid
  • Only the owner can rename it, delete bills, or add and remove people
  • History → Activity shows who added, updated or paid each bill, with their name and photo, and exactly what changed
  • Each bill shows who added it and who updated it last
  • Share one phone? Everyone stays signed in and switches accounts in one tap, no password

Install on iPhone

On your home screen in about a minute

Open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the new icon, and turn on reminders in Settings.

  1. Open bills-and-dues.web.app in Safari (iOS 16.4 or newer)
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add
  3. Open it from the icon and sign in
  4. Tap your initials → Enable on this device → Allow

The small things

Made for how bills work here

Everything in pesos

Amounts in ₱, with the monthly cost of each category. Quarterly and yearly bills are spread per month, so the total is honest.

Loans and installments

See the balance left, the months to go, and the payoff date, for loans, credit cards and appliance installments. Don't know the balance? Enter what you've paid, or just the last payment date.

Add your own, like Pets or School, with an icon. Your own categories can track a remaining balance, for installments like a new appliance.

Safe to clean up

Delete a single payment, and when you delete a bill, choose whether to keep its payments so your totals stay right.

On your home screen

No app store. Add it from the browser and it opens like an app, with a badge for bills overdue or due today.

Light and dark

Tap the moon at the top for dark mode, or let it follow your phone's setting.
